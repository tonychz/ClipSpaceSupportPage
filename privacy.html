Privacy Policy

Clipboard Copy Paste & Files stores your clips and settings on your device. If you enable iCloud Sync, your clips are also stored and synchronized through your iCloud account.


We use RevenueCat, a third-party subscription service, to validate purchases and manage subscription entitlements. RevenueCat processes purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier associated with the subscription. We do not link this information to your identity, use it to track you across apps or websites, sell it, or use it for advertising.


Your clipboard items are not sent to RevenueCat or any other third party. Clipboard Sharing works only between devices you choose on your local network.
